## Language & Script

- The Mundari people call their language **horo jagar** — "the people's speech". It belongs to the Austroasiatic family (ISO code: **unr**).
- Its own script, **Mundari Bani**, was created by **Rohidas Singh Nag**. The modern script has **27 letters** and entered the world's digital standard with **Unicode 15.0**.
- Mundari counts **in twenties** — a vigesimal counting system, carried on the site's numbers page exactly as the community counts.
