Help Preserve Our Heritage

Do you know a traditional song, a local story, a Mundari word, a historical fact or a family photograph that should be documented? Share it with us. Every contribution is reviewed before publication — nothing becomes public automatically.

How submission works (honestly): This is a static website with no server or database, so this form does not store or send anything by itself. When you press the button, it opens your own email app with your contribution addressed to mundarisamaj@outlook.com — you press Send there. For photos or recordings, attach them in that email.
