Festivals & Celebrations

Festivals & Seasonal Celebrations

A festival directory — researched and verified festival by festival. Not every festival is celebrated by every Munda community, or in the same way; each entry names the communities and regions where it is documented. Every festival now has its own page — open a card to read it in full.

Photo: Rohitmahali01 · CC0 1.0 · via Wikimedia Commons

Sarhul

The festival of flowers, with Sarna and the akhra. Season and practices vary by region.

Open the Sarhul page →Photo: 18susmita · CC BY-SA 4.0 · via Wikimedia Commons

Karam / Karma

Young people bring a Karam branch and sing and dance all night. Local names and observances vary.

Open the Karam page →Photo: Shreyashprashu · CC BY-SA 4.0 · via Wikimedia Commons

Sohrai

Lamps, decorated cattle sheds and wall painting, in the seasonal cycle.

Open the Sohrai page →Photo: Noukayami · CC BY-SA 4.0 · via Wikimedia Commons

Mage Parab

The household festival of the ancestors, kept at the Paush full moon, when the winter harvest is in.

Open the Mage Parab page →Photo: Dev0745 · CC BY 4.0 · via Wikimedia Commons

Phagu

The festival of the ritual hunt — burning trees, the Pahan’s offering, and the village hunt at the Phalgun full moon.

Open the Phagu page →
