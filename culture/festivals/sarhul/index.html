Sarhul

The festival of flowers — when new sal blossoms are offered at the Sarna grove, and the new year and spring are welcomed.

𞓛𞓕𞓣𞓞𞓟𞓒Sarhul

March–April · the festival of flowers

New sal flowers are offered at the Sarna grove; the new year and spring are welcomed. Season and practices vary by region.

The setting — Sarna

The traditional religion of the Munda community is Sarna — its centre is the sacred grove of trees near the village (the sarna / jaher), not a temple building. The village priest, the 𞓑𞓕𞓞𞓕𞓨 Pahan, worships the village deities (𞓞𞓕𞓝𞓟 𞓗𞓐𞓔𞓦𞓕𞓢𞓐 Hatu Boŋgako) and the spirits of the ancestors. The supreme deity is 𞓛𞓚𞓔𞓗𞓐𞓔𞓦𞓕 Singbonga — the Sun god.

A translation warning: 𞓗𞓐𞓔𞓦𞓕 boŋga means deity / spirit / power — do not simply replace it with the monotheistic word “God”, and never call a Sarna grove a “temple”.
