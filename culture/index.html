Munda culture is lived, not displayed — in the akhra where the village dances, at the sarna grove where the Pahan prays, and in the turning of the agricultural year. Every festival and custom below has its own page, told in full.

Nothing here is repeated anywhere else on this site: each card opens the one home of that topic.

Festivals

The turning points of the Munda year — tap any festival to read its full story.

Sarhul

The festival of flowers — new sal blossoms offered at the Sarna grove, welcoming the new year and spring.

Karam

Young people bring a Karam branch and sing and dance all night, wishing prosperity for the village.

Mage Parab

The great winter festival on the full moon of Pous — the month the Mundas call Mage — when the harvest is in.

Sohrai

Lamps are lit, the cattle shed is washed and decorated, and the walls bloom with Sohrai painting.

Phagu

The festival of the ritual hunt, kept at the full moon of Phalgun — the season of Holi.
