Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Mundari Samaj, the Mundari language and this website.

What is Mundari Samaj?

Mundari Samaj is a community platform dedicated to preserving and promoting the Mundari language, the Mundari Bani script, and the history, culture and living traditions of the Munda people. It is a project of Catvers Studio.

What is the Mundari Bani script?

Mundari Bani is the script created for the Mundari language by Rohidas Singh Nag. Its modern form has 27 letters, and it was added to Unicode in 2022 (Unicode 15.0), so it can be typed and read on phones and computers. You can see the full alphabet chart on our Script page.

How many people speak Mundari?

According to the Census of India 2011, 1,634,150 people were recorded under Mundari and Munda together (1,128,228 as "Mundari" and 505,922 as "Munda"). Mundari is listed by UNESCO as a Vulnerable language, which is one reason this archive exists.

Is Mundari an official language?

Mundari has been an additional official language of Jharkhand since 2011. Its inclusion in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India is a long-standing demand of the community.

How can I start learning Mundari here?

Start with the Learn Mundari page for flashcards and everyday sentences, the Dictionary for words with their Bani script, and Numbers & Counting to see how Mundari counts in twenties.

Are the books on this site free to read?

Yes. The Books section collects public-domain and government-published Mundari books and textbooks — including CIIL primers and classic grammars — and every book can be read in the browser or downloaded, free of charge.

How can I contribute a story, word or photograph?

Use the Contribute page or write to mundarisamaj@outlook.com. Everything shared with us is reviewed before it is published, and contributors are credited.

Who runs this website?

The website is run by Catvers Studio. You can reach us by email at mundarisamaj@outlook.com, on Instagram at @mundarisamaj and on Threads at @mundaricommunity.
