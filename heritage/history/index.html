History & Heritage

Stories That Connect Generations

Munda history is studied from written records, archives, language evidence and oral histories — and these sources do not always agree. This hub distinguishes documented facts, scholarly interpretations and community accounts, and names the region and source wherever accounts differ. There is no single origin story presented here as settled fact.
