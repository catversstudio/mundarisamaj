Identity

Identity

An ancient language, from the soil of Chota Nagpur

Mundari is a North Munda (Kherwarian) language of the Austroasiatic family — the closest relative of Santali and Ho. Its own name is 𞓞𞓐𞓪𞓐 𞓖𞓕𞓦𞓕𞓣 horo jagar; the word “Munda” itself means “village headman” in Mundari.

Jharkhand

The largest home

Khunti, Ranchi, Seraikela-Kharsawan and Singhbhum — 57.6% of all speakers in India lived here in 2011.

Odisha 29.5%

The Mayurbhanj centre

Mayurbhanj, Keonjhar, Sundargarh, Balasore. Mayurbhanj is also a major centre of Mundari Bani publishing (Burubanga / Baripada).

3 countries

Across borders too

About 2,500 speakers in Bangladesh (Naogaon, Rajshahi) and about 7,700 in Nepal's Koshi region; within India there are communities in West Bengal and Assam as well.

ISO unr

The identity code

ISO 639-3 code unr, Glottolog mund1320. Note — the “Mandari” of South Sudan (mqu) is a completely different language.

Status and demand — understand the difference: Mundari is an additional official language of Jharkhand (since 2011). But it is not yet included in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution — that is the community's long-standing, living demand, not a status already granted. See this week's movement on that demand on the Movement page.

The four dialects

DialectWhere / whoWhat is special
𞓞𞓕𞓛𞓕𞓡𞓕HasadaEast of the Ranchi–Chaibasa roadRegarded as the standard dialect; Hoffmann's Encyclopaedia Mundarica is based on it
𞓨𞓕𞓦𞓟𞓣𞓚NaguriWest of the same roadHoffmann's Mundari Grammar (1903) is based on it; aspirated sounds are absorbed into the dialect
𞓝𞓕𞓧𞓕𞓣𞓚𞓕Tamaria (𞓒𞓕𞓝𞓕𞓣 Latar)Panchpargana — Tamar, Bundu, Silli, Rahe, SonahatuCalled the “lowland dialect”; Bhumij is often considered a form of it
𞓢𞓤𞓣𞓕KeraRanchi city area, among Oraon (Kurukh) speakersIts past-tense ending is -𞓢𞓤𞓣𞓕𞓙 -keraʔ, where the others have -𞓢𞓤𞓡𞓕 -keda — that is its identity mark

Bhumij (Mayurbhanj–Singhbhum) is listed as a separate language in many lists, but Anderson (2008) and Osada treat it as a form of Mundari's Tamaria/Latar dialect — most Bhumij material is useful for a learner, but check it against Hasada.

How Mundari differs from Santali and Ho

MundariSantaliHo

Mutual understandingThe three Kherwarian languages are mutually intelligible to a large extent (Ho teachers in Mayurbhanj reported about 75% comprehension), but by community, script and standardisation they are three separate languages — do not use a Santali or Ho word in place of a Mundari one without checking.
