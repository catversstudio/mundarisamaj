The demand is simple, and it is alive: Mundari should take its place in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution, and in Odisha it should be recognised as a second official language. Mundari is already an additional official language of Jharkhand (since 2011) — the movement asks that the recognition be completed.

The Mundari Language Co-ordination Committee (MLCC) carries this demand village by village and block by block, in writing: memorandums submitted to the administration, signed by the community.
