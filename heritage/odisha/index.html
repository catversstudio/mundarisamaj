Mundari in Odisha

Research in progress for Odisha

  • History of Munda people in Mayurbhanj — district-level research in progress
  • Munda folklore in Odisha — tales are being verified one by one against original publications before publication
  • Village histories from Mayurbhanj, Sundargarh and Keonjhar — to be built with community contributors

Sources for this collection

  • Odisha State Tribal Museum — separate Munda and Mundari profiles.
  • SCST Research and Training Institute, Government of Odisha — community profiles and demographic publications.
  • Census of India tables — always quoted with year, district and exact category.

A note on names: official records use labels such as Munda, Mundari and Kolha in different ways in different tables. This collection keeps those categories separate and says which source uses which term.
