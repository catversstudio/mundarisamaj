Birsa Munda

Leader of the Ulgulan (1899–1900), the great Munda uprising for land and self-rule, whose pressure brought the Chotanagpur Tenancy Act, 1908. Read his full biography — family, schooling, faith, prison, rebellion and legacy, with sources.

Rohidas Singh Nag

Creator of the Mundari Bani script: creation begun in 1949 when he was a schoolboy in Mayurbhanj; 35 characters published 1953; simplified 27-letter form 1980; 2008 reform entered Unicode 15.0 (2022).
