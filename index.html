Every letter carries a story

Mundari Bani has 27 letters, created by Rohidas Singh Nag. Tap any letter to see its name and Roman sound.

The script was begun in 1949, simplified to 27 letters in 1980, and joined Unicode in 2022. The full chart, the Leneka digits and the Tong marks are on the script page.
