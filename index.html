Who we are

A community. A language. A legacy.

Mundari Samaj is a community platform dedicated to the Mundari people and the Mundari language. We believe every language holds a world of knowledge, memory and belonging.

𞓞𞓐𞓣𞓐 𞓖𞓕𞓦𞓕𞓣 — “Horo Jagar”, the language of the people.

Our mission is to help preserve Mundari language and culture in the digital world, making space for stories, learning and connection across generations.

Our written heritage

Every letter carries a story.

Mundari has its own script — Mundari Bani, created by Rohidas Singh Nag. Its 27 letters are an important part of the language’s written identity, and sharing knowledge of the script helps more people discover, read and celebrate Mundari. Tap any letter to open the full script page.
