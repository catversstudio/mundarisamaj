Mundari — 𞓞𞓐𞓪𞓐 𞓖𞓕𞓦𞓕𞓣 horo jagar, "the language of the people" — is the mother tongue of the Munda community, spoken across the Chota Nagpur plateau in Jharkhand and in the Mayurbhanj heartland of Odisha. It is an additional official language of Jharkhand, and UNESCO lists it as vulnerable — every learner and every speaker keeps it alive.

This section is the doorway to the language itself. Each part below gives you a taste here and its full room on its own page.
