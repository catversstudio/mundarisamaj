Language

The Mundari Language

One of the central sections of this platform. Mundari (ISO 639-3: unr; Glottolog: mund1320) belongs to the North Munda branch of the Austroasiatic family. Its word order is SOV, it counts in twenties (vigesimal), and it has two words for “we” — one including the listener, one excluding. Every Mundari word on this platform is shown in Mundari Bani script beside its Roman form.

Script — Mundari Bani

The full 27-letter alphabet chart, digits and tone marks. Script created by Rohidas Singh Nag: begun 1949, 35 characters in 1953, simplified to 27 letters in 1980; the 2008 reform is the Unicode version (Unicode 15.0, 2022, U+1E4D0–U+1E4FF).
