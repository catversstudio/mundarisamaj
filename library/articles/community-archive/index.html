Building a respectful community archive

2026-11-02 · 4 min read · Culture · Mundari Samaj Editorial Desk

A community archive can bring together photographs, documents, recordings and stories that help people explore their heritage. The value of an archive depends on how material was collected and described.

Ask permission and give credit

Before publishing a photograph, recording or document, confirm who owns it and whether the project has permission to display it. Credit the creator or contributor in the way they request.
