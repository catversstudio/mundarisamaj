Birsa Munda (1875–1900) — Dharti Aaba, Father of the Earth

Birsa Munda (1875–1900), revered as Dharti Aaba — “Father of the Earth” — led the Ulgulan, the great Munda uprising of 1899–1900, against colonial rule and the loss of tribal land. His full story: family, schooling, faith, prison, rebellion and legacy, compiled from published histories.

Rohidas Singh Nag (1934–2012) — Ol Guru, creator of Mundari Bani

Rohidas Singh Nag (1934–2012), revered as Ol Guru, gave the Mundari language its own script. As a class III schoolboy in 1949 he wrote the first Mundari Bani letters in clay on his school wall; the script he shaped across his lifetime entered Unicode in 2022 — and it is the script beside every Mundari word on this website.

Jaipal Singh Munda (1903–1970) — Marang Gomke, the Great Leader

Jaipal Singh Munda captained India to its first Olympic hockey gold, spoke for millions of Adivasis in the Constituent Assembly — “I am proud to be a Jungli” — and founded the Jharkhand Party to demand a homeland for his people.

The Munda Clan (Killi) System

As recorded by S. C. Roy in 1912, the Munda tribe was divided into a large number of exogamous clans called kilis — brotherhoods whose members traced descent from one common ancestor, stood by one another in weal and woe, and married only outside the clan.
