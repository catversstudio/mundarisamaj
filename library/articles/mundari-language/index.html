Mundari is a language of the Munda communities, with speakers across parts of eastern and central India and beyond. Like every living language, it carries ways of describing relationships, land, work, memory and the world.

A language lives through its speakers

Language preservation is not only about recording words. It also means supporting everyday use, intergenerational learning, songs, oral histories, local expressions and the many contexts in which people communicate.

Document with care

Spelling, pronunciation and usage can vary by region and speaker. A responsible language resource should record who shared a word, where it is used, how it is pronounced and whether a native-speaker review has taken place.
