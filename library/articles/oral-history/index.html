Why oral histories matter

2026-11-02 · 4 min read · Heritage · Mundari Samaj Editorial Desk

An oral history is a recorded account of a person's memories and experiences. It can help future generations understand how people remember places, livelihoods, celebrations, change and community life.

Listen before recording

Explain how a recording may be used, ask what should remain private, and allow the speaker to pause or withdraw permission. Do not assume every story is intended for public sharing.
