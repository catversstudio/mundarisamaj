Mundari Grammar (1903)

John Hoffmann S.J. · Bengal Secretariat Press, Calcutta · English

The first full grammar of the Mundari language, written by Rev. John Hoffmann S.J. and published in Calcutta in 1903 — the foundation work for all later Mundari language study. Public-domain scan, free to read and download.
