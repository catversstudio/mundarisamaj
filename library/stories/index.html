The Asur Legend — how Sing Bonga outwitted the iron-smelters

The best-loved of the old Munda legends: the Asurs whose furnaces scorched the earth, the boy with the itching sores who was Sing Bonga in disguise, and how the bongas of hill and forest came to be.

Karma and Dharma — the legend of Karam Raja

The origin legend of the Karma festival, as told at Jojohatu: how dancing came down from Indra's heaven, how Karma lost everything the night he cut down the Karam branches, and how he crossed forest and river to find Karam Raja floating on the water.
