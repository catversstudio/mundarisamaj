Adi Vaani grows: full platform launched at the Mysuru tribal languages conclave

2026-08-24 · Language technology

Adi Vaani, the Government of India's AI-powered translation platform for tribal languages, moved from beta to a full platform at the National Conclave on Tribal Languages and Museums in Mysuru, inaugurated by Minister of State for Tribal Affairs Durgadas Uikey. The upgraded platform adds an iOS app, five additional languages (taking the total to nine), a new logo, a “Jago” AI conversational assistant for the Ministry, and a memorandum of understanding with the Central Institute of Indian Languages (CIIL), Mysuru, for tribal-language documentation and corpus development. Mundari — one of the four original beta languages — remains at the heart of the platform, which the Ministry describes as an effort to preserve and promote India's tribal languages. Sources: Akashvani News and The Times of India, reporting the Ministry's releases (PIB PRID 2302927, 2302944).
