Memorandums in Khunta and GB Nagar demand Eighth Schedule status for Mundari

2026-10-28 · Language movement

On 28 October 2026, delegations of the Mundari Language Co-ordination Committee (MLCC) in Khunta Block and Gopabandhu Nagar (GB Nagar) Block submitted demand memorandums to their Block Development Officers. The memorandums carry two demands: the inclusion of the Mundari language in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India, and the recognition of Mundari as the second official language of Odisha. Photographs shared by Lachhuman Singh show the delegation, in white traditional dress, presenting the memorandum inside the block office and outside the building with the committee banner. It is important to be clear: these are demands presented to officials — they have not been granted. The submissions were part of a wider wave of Mundari language-movement activity across Mayurbhanj district in the last week of October 2026, also covered in this site's Movement section.
