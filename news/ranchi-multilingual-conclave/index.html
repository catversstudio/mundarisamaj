Jharkhand minister calls for tribal languages to anchor primary education

2026-01-07 · Education

On 7 January 2026, Jharkhand's Higher and Technical Education Minister Sudivya Kumar inaugurated a two-day National Multilingual Education Conclave in Ranchi, held under the National Education Policy, and said that tribal and regional languages must be included in primary education. Officials at the conclave confirmed that the PALASH programme currently teaches in the state's five tribal languages — including Mundari — in 1,041 schools across eight districts, and discussed adding Jharkhand's four regional languages (Khortha, Nagpuri, Panchpargania and Kurmali) to early schooling as well. The conclave signals that mother-tongue education, Mundari included, is set to expand further in Jharkhand. Source: PTI report in The Print, 7 January 2026.
