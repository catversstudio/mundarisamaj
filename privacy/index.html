Privacy Policy

What this website collects, what it does not, and the choices you have.
Operated by: Catvers Studio · Contact: mundarisamaj@outlook.com
Effective Date: November 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mundari Samaj, a digital community archive dedicated to preserving and promoting the Mundari language, culture, traditions, history, and heritage.

Our website provides access to educational and cultural resources, including dictionaries, books, articles, traditional stories, photographs, videos, and community news.

Mundari Samaj is operated by Catvers Studio.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Visitors can browse and access publicly available content without creating an account.

Certain interactive features, such as liking, commenting, and following, require an account.

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name: Your account display name.
  • Email Address: For account authentication and identification.
  • Username: Your selected public username.
  • Profile Photo: An optional profile image with a maximum size of 20 KB.

We collect only the information necessary to provide the website's account-related features.

3. Community Activities

When registered users interact with our website, certain activities are stored, including:

  • Comments.
  • Likes.
  • Follows.

Comments may become publicly visible after submission. We use automated content moderation to help prevent inappropriate comments.

Visitors can report comments they believe are inappropriate.

Please avoid including personal, confidential, or sensitive information in public comments.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected through our website may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide community interaction features.
  • Display public comments and usernames.
  • Maintain likes and follows.
  • Count video views and interactions.
  • Maintain website functionality.
  • Improve the visitor experience.
  • Respond to user requests and reports.

We do not sell personal information or use it for commercial advertising.

5. Browser Storage and Cookies

Our website uses browser storage technologies to maintain essential website functionality and remember visitor preferences.

This may include:

  • Login session information.
  • Privacy and analytics preferences.
  • Video preferences.
  • Technical identifiers used for counting interactions.

You can clear stored website data through your browser settings. This may affect login sessions and saved preferences.

6. Analytics and Visitor Consent

We use Google Analytics to understand general website usage and improve our services.

Google Analytics is activated only when a visitor accepts analytics through the website's consent banner.

Visitors who do not accept analytics consent will not have this consent-controlled analytics feature activated.

Your consent preference is remembered by your browser.

Analytics information is handled according to the applicable policies of Google and our consent practices.

7. Third-Party Services

Mundari Samaj uses external services to support website functionality and display content.

These may include services for:

  • Account authentication.
  • Website infrastructure and data storage.
  • Image hosting.
  • Document viewing.
  • Video and social media embedding.
  • Website analytics.

Our website may display embedded videos from YouTube and posts from Instagram. Books and documents may open through Google Drive's document viewer.

When you interact with external services or embedded content, those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of these external platforms.

8. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information and maintain the privacy and integrity of our website.

Access to personal information is limited to authorized purposes.

However, no online platform or method of electronic storage can guarantee complete security. Users should avoid sharing passwords, verification codes, or confidential information with anyone.

9. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the website and provide its features.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or valid legal obligations.

We do not share personal information for third-party advertising purposes.

10. Advertisements and Payments

Mundari Samaj is a community-focused, non-commercial archive.

Currently:

  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not collect payments.
  • We do not offer paid subscriptions.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Our primary purpose is the preservation and promotion of Mundari language, culture, and heritage.

11. Your Privacy Rights and Data Deletion

You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal information.

To submit a request, contact us using our official email address: mundarisamaj@outlook.com

Please provide your registered email address and username so that we can verify your request.

After verification, we will take reasonable steps to process your request and delete eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

Deleting browser data alone does not delete information already stored on our servers.

12. Administrative Access

Website administration is restricted to authorized personnel.

Administrative access is used only for legitimate website management, content maintenance, and moderation purposes.

We do not publish internal administrative credentials, access information, or security configurations.

13. Children's Privacy

Mundari Samaj is an educational and cultural resource accessible to the general public.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from children.

Parents or guardians who have concerns about personal information associated with a child may contact us to request appropriate action.

14. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites and online resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy policies, security practices, or content of external websites.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of external services before providing them with personal information.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in our website, services, or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.
