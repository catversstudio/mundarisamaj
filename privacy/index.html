Privacy Policy

What this website collects, what it does not, and the choices you have. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Who we are

Mundari Samaj (mundarisamaj.pages.dev) is a community website run by Catvers Studio. For any privacy question, write to mundarisamaj@outlook.com.

What we do not collect

Visitors do not create accounts on this website, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or address to read anything here. The Contribute form does not store anything on this website — it simply opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us, and nothing is sent until you press Send there.

Analytics (only with your consent)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read and how the site can improve. Analytics stays off until you press "Accept" on the cookie banner; if you press "Decline", no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google Analytics may collect your device and browser type, the pages you visit, how long you spend on them, and your approximate location (country and region). We never see anything that identifies you personally. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings — the banner will ask again on your next visit.

Hosting

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like every hosting provider, Cloudflare keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) for security and reliable delivery.

Embedded content from other platforms

Some pages show YouTube videos and Instagram posts inside official embedded players. When you play or interact with them, those platforms (Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram) may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies.

The admin area

The /admin area is used only by the website's administrator to manage content. It uses a sign-in cookie that exists only for that purpose; visitors never receive it.

Contact

If you would like anything about you removed from this website, or you have any other privacy question, email mundarisamaj@outlook.com and we will help.
